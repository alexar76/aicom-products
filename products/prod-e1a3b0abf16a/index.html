FAQ

What is a skeptic pass?

A structured human review checklist covering claims, sources, tone, and risk. It ensures every AI‑generated draft gets a real human look before it reaches a client.

Is the share page indexed by search engines?

No. Share pages are no‑index, no‑follow by default. They are meant for private client sharing, not public SEO.

Can I white‑label the share page?

Yes, on the Agency plan you can remove all Relay branding and use your own domain via CNAME.

What does the JSON receipt include?

Handoff ID, timestamps, operator email, verification checklist results, approval state, content SHA‑256 hash, and the public share URL — everything a compliance team needs.

How does the embed work?

Copy a one‑line <script> snippet into any HTML page. The badge loads asynchronously, shows your workspace name and “Human‑verified”, and links to the full share page.

Sentinel (670)

Sentinel is an LLM-free, embeddable safety companion that answers 'is it safe here right now?' for weather, wildfire, and flood hazards.

For Website owners and operators in travel & hospitality, outdoor recreation, real estate, and event management; secondary: local news, insurance, public agencies, and enterprises with distributed safety needs.

What you get

✦

Embeddable safety widget

A single script tag that renders a location-aware safety badge/page on any website with no iframe chrome and no backend setup.

◆

Location handling with privacy

Manual city/coordinate entry as default, optional browser geolocation with consent, coordinates rounded to ~1 decimal before persistence.

●

ATLAS capability invokes

Invokes atlas.situation.brief@v1, atlas.fire.weather@v1, atlas.nearest.read@v1, persists receipt digests, and degrades gracefully.

▲

Deterministic rule engine

No LLM at runtime; maps evidence to CALM/WATCH/WARNING/EMERGENCY per hazard plus overall level.

★

Signed evidence receipts

Every advisory displays a receipt digest linking to the signed ATLAS response; never show an advisory without a receipt.
